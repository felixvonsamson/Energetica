# Wiki - Progression

As your energy system grows, you earn **experience points (XP)**. XP measures how far you have come in the game. You earn it in two ways: by reaching achievement milestones and by answering the daily quiz correctly. The leaderboards let you compare your XP and other statistics with every other player on the server.

## Achievements and XP

Achievements track your progress in different parts of the game. You can follow them in the achievement progression section of the [dashboard](/app/dashboard). Each achievement has a series of milestones, and reaching a milestone gives you the XP shown next to it.

### Unlock Achievements

Four achievements unlock new parts of the game. Each gives 1 XP.

- **Unlock Network**: reach a power consumption of 3 MW. You can then join or create a [network](./network.mdx) and manage your [power priorities](./power-management.mdx).
- **Unlock Technologies**: build the [laboratory](./functional-facilities.mdx#the-laboratory).
- **Unlock Natural Resources**: build the [warehouse](./functional-facilities.mdx#the-warehouse).
- **First Storage Facility**: build your first [storage facility](./storage-facilities.mdx).

### Milestone Achievements

The other achievements have several milestones, and later milestones give more XP. An achievement only appears once you have unlocked what it depends on. For example, the Technology achievement appears once you have built the laboratory.

| Achievement        | Measures                                      | Appears after                      | Milestones (XP)                                             |
| ------------------ | --------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------- |
| Power Consumption  | Your highest power consumption                | From the start                     | 10 MW (5), 150 MW (10), 6.5 GW (15), 100 GW (20), 3 TW (25) |
| Energy Storage     | The most energy you have had stored at once   | First Storage Facility             | 8 GWh (5), 160 GWh (10), 5 TWh (20)                         |
| Mineral Extraction | Total resources extracted                     | Natural Resources and Technologies | 500 t (5), 10 kt (10), 200 kt (15)                          |
| Network Import     | Total energy bought on the network            | Network                            | 10 GWh (5), 200 GWh (10), 4 TWh (15)                        |
| Network Export     | Total energy sold on the network              | Network                            | 10 GWh (5), 200 GWh (10), 4 TWh (15)                        |
| Technology         | Total technology levels researched            | Technologies                       | 10 (5), 25 (10), 50 (15), 100 (20)                          |
| Resource Export    | Total resources sold on the resource market   | Natural Resources                  | 200 t (5), 5 kt (10), 100 kt (15)                           |
| Resource Import    | Total resources bought on the resource market | Natural Resources                  | 200 t (5), 5 kt (10), 100 kt (15)                           |

## Daily Quiz

Every day at 09:00 UTC, a new multiple-choice question about energy and climate appears on the [daily quiz](/app/dashboard/quiz) page, which you can open from the dashboard. You can answer each question once. A correct answer gives you 1 XP. After you answer, the quiz shows the correct answer with a short explanation and a link to learn more.

## Leaderboards

The [leaderboards](/app/community/leaderboards) list every player on the server. Click a column header to sort by that column, for example by XP to see who has progressed the furthest. The category buttons switch between sets of statistics:

- **General**: network, highest power consumption, operating income, number of projects, technology levels and XP.
- **Power & Energy**: energy exported and imported on the network, and the most energy stored at once.
- **Resources**: resources extracted, sold and bought.
- **Technologies**: the level of each technology, and the total.
- **Functional Facilities**: the levels of the industry, the laboratory and the warehouse.
- **Emissions**: net CO<sub>2</sub> emissions and captured CO<sub>2</sub>.
